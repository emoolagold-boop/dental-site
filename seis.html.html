Tax-Efficient Investment for Dental Professionals

The Opportunity

As a dental practice owner, you're likely paying 40% or 45% income tax on a significant portion of your earnings. The government's Seed Enterprise Investment Scheme (SEIS) allows you to invest up to £200,000 per year in qualifying UK companies and claim 50% of that investment back as income tax relief.

Your actual downside on a £50,000 investment is approximately £13,750 after all tax reliefs (assuming additional-rate taxpayer). Your accountant can confirm these figures for your individual circumstances.
